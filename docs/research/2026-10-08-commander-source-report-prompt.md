concepts.The Command Zone (Sources 1, 2, & 4) Terminology:Vegetables: The foundational support cards required for a deck

Interaction).Plan / Meat: Cards representing the deck's primary strategy and win conditions. Maps to Deck
Packages.Enablers: Cards that perform the foundational actions needed to make the plan work (e.g., token generators,
sacrifice outlets). Maps to Generators/Enablers.Enhancers: Force multipliers (like doubling seasons or damage doublers)
that drastically improve the plan but do absolutely nothing on their own. Maps to Amplifiers.Payoffs: Cards that reward
the execution of the plan or win the game outright (e.g., Blood Artist for dying creatures, Craterhoof Behemoth for wide
boards). Maps to Payoffs.Magical Christmas Land: A derogatory term for a game state that requires too many perfect,
sequential steps and zero opponent interaction to pull off.Commander Challenge (Source 3) Terminology:Keystone
Objective: The deck's core focus, broken into three distinct parts: the primary action, how the deck capitalizes on that
action, and the definitive win condition. Maps to Deck Plan.Advantage Gainers: The supporting pillars of Card Advantage,
Mana Advantage, and Board State Advantage (Suppression/Protection).
