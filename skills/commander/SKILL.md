# Commander

Apply the shared `deck-development`, `card-research` and `probability-analysis` methods as needed. Respect Commander Bracket, Color Identity, singleton construction, command-zone eligibility and the confirmed play experience. Inspect exact commander text, costs, timing and dependencies. Command-zone access is separate from library draws; evaluate how the Mainboard operates through removal, commander tax and disruption. Commander has no ordinary Sideboard.

## The Template

The Template is the adopted The Command Zone Podcast Commander starting profile: 38 land/mana-access slots, 12 card advantage, 10 ramp, 12 targeted disruption, 6 mass disruption and approximately 30 plan-serving cards. Functions overlap and do not sum to deck size. Define what qualifies, account for actual selected quantities and explain a justified departure in its focused reasoning. Assess whether the resulting plan still works under the Brief. Treat selection separately from net advantage, temporary mana separately from repeatable ramp, and protection separately from removal. The adopted mana-access convention can include MDFCs and one-mana landcyclers; explain their competing uses and timing, distinct from deterministic land counts.

For example, a recursion deck may count conditional graveyard access separately from unconditional draw and deliberately choose fewer sweepers to protect its own board, while acknowledging exposure to large boards. A modal card supplies only the mode used at a time. These counts and labels do not prove adequacy; inspect causal conditions and competing resources.
