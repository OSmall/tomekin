# Card research

1. Translate the anchor's actions and ordinary support needs into mechanisms, prerequisites, enabling events and independent Oracle/property criteria. Study useful Research Exemplars even when they are not eligible for selection; that permission does not authorize their inclusion.
2. Inspect exemplar Oracle facts and direct/inherited tags, then use `browse_tags` to learn actual complete meanings. The default definition gives slug and nullable description; select metadata or hierarchy when needed. A missing definition, not-found or unready dataset is not a confirmed meaning: use independent criteria or other evidence instead of inventing one.
3. Search complementary mechanisms and ordinary support across the permitted scope. Preserve native search query and controls. Use explicit narrow facts for broad exploration, then batch missing Oracle facts for promising options. Apply Collection and selection constraints to the right function; permitted scoped search evidence establishes membership, not final joint Availability. New discoveries can revise queries, graph and reasoning.
4. Traverse every page of each search used: repeat fixed inputs and effective selectors, forward the actual `next_cursor` as `after` until null, and preserve delivered facts and safe `error.cursor` on partial failure. A decision that another branch is unlikely to change choices may stop new searches, not abandon pages of one already used. Changed inputs or relevant baseline require restart; do not mix traversals. A first of zero consumes no records. Report incomplete research and recovery position.
5. Compare contextual alternatives, qualifying costs, timing, repeatability and resource competition; assess coverage of identified mechanisms, support and unexplored branches. Neither a large pool nor selection readiness proves exhaustive research or optimality. Retain substantive preparation in the candidate at stage boundaries as directed by `deck-development`.

Use `lookup_cards` for exact names/identities or Printings with selected facts, `search_cards` for native provider discovery, `browse_collection_cards` for actual permitted rows, and `browse_tags` for definitions. Tools report unready/unknown separately from known empty. Use complete returned facts rather than silently clipping a large record. For native Pi composition and same-session checkpoints read [codemode.md](codemode.md). When the explicitly enabled legacy local Card Query is required, read [legacy-query.md](legacy-query.md); its policy limitations differ from provider scoped discovery.
