# 60-card Constructed

Use `deck-development` for the requested workflow, `card-research` for investigation and `probability-analysis` for numerical decisions. Supported Formats include Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper and Casual 60; retrieve the actual Format constraints and legality. An open archetype can begin with Collection-backed discovery; an Existing Deck begins with its requested diagnosis.

For tuning, distinguish focused repair, rebuilding around an identity and fresh construction. Obtain agreement before expanding focused repair into either broader task.

## Develop the list

1. Describe the concrete plan and plausible early, middle and closing turns. Identify enablers, payoffs, interaction, protection and recovery. An anchor in the Mainboard must be drawn, searched for or otherwise accessed; explain how the deck functions with and without it. Choose colors from the plan and viable mana.
2. Choose copy counts by useful deadline and the value of repeated draws. More copies improve access but can diminish in value for legendary, expensive or situational cards. A broad role may contain several distinct cards; distinguish access to any role contributor from access to a required combination. Use `probability-analysis` for material quantity decisions.
3. Usually aim for 60 Mainboard cards for consistency; explain a larger legal list. Check actual copy limits across applicable sections, including card-specific overrides. After allocating lands and support, confirm that enough slots remain to execute and finish the plan.
4. Review functional curve and mana together: actual costs, colored requirements, untapped access, ramp installation and competing spending. Distinguish selection from net card advantage. Replace the weakest included nonlands or explain their specific role, dependency or Brief constraint. Finish with a reasoned static assessment; claim games or simulations only when actually performed.

An optional reference land estimate from the adopted 60-card material is `19.59 + 1.90 × average nonland mana value − 0.28 × cheap draw/ramp count + 0.27 × companion count`. It has substantial unexplained variation. Compare its estimate with the actual curve, colored sources, modal lands, tempo and intended turns before choosing a land count; it is neither a legality rule nor a quality score.

## Requested Sideboard

Add a Sideboard only when requested and applicable. Name plausible matchups, the proposed cards in and out, and their effect on the plan. Verify that each exchange preserves required functions and respects Format constraints. Keep the Sideboard outside Mainboard sampling calculations unless the question explicitly models a post-sideboard configuration.
