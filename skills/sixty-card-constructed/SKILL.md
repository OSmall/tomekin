# 60-card Constructed

Apply the shared `deck-development`, `card-research` and `probability-analysis` methods without assuming a commander. Supported Formats include Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper and Casual 60; use actual Format constraints and legality, not a blanket rule for every Format. An open archetype can begin with Collection-backed discovery; an Existing Deck begins with its requested diagnosis. Choose colors from the plan and viable mana, not the reverse.

Construct around coherent enablers, payoffs, interaction and support; compare effects in context and choose repeated quantities by useful deadline and whether multiples help. Respect actual copy limits, including card-specific overrides. Usually aim for 60 Mainboard cards for consistency; a larger legal list needs a stated reason. Add a Sideboard only when requested and applicable; describe matchup assumptions, cards in and out and impact on the plan.

Review early, middle and closing turns, functional curve, actual colored sources, tapped lands, ramp timing, card advantage and dependencies together. Distinguish selection from net advantage. Consider the weakest included nonlands: replace them or explain their specific role, dependency or Brief constraint. Perform a static review; unsupported playtesting or simulation must not be claimed.

For a 60-card opening hand with four qualifying Mainboard copies, seven uniformly sampled cards and no mulligans, search or prior information, the chance of at least one is approximately 39.95%. This is access, not castability. The applicable Sideboard is outside that sample. Use actual selected quantities and the shared probability method for each decision. An optional starting land estimate from the adopted 60-card material is `19.59 + 1.90 × average nonland mana value - 0.28 × cheap draw/ramp count + 0.27 × companion count`; it has substantial unexplained variation and needs contextual adjustments for curve, sources, modal lands and tempo. It is neither legality nor a quality score.
