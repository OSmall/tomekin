# Deck-Shape Methodology

Compose `tomekin-deck-building` for lifecycle and supported tools, `query-cards` for retrieval mechanics, and the
applicable Format architecture/tuning skill for assumptions and examples. Scale this sequence to the request: a
nominated-card review needs the affected functions, not a compulsory full-deck audit.

## Reason From Context To Configuration

1. **Context.** Read the confirmed Brief and relevant current plan, selected membership, pool assessments and evidence.
   For discovery or fresh construction, establish the direction first. Reuse established user intent; state material
   assumptions and respect the Addition Pool and Collection evidence boundary.
2. **Plan.** Explain repeatable activities/resources, how they create advantage, and primary/secondary Win Routes.
   Identify the Format Anchor's actual contributions and what happens when access is disrupted. Review dependency
   burden and complementary routes in context; prose-step count is not a quality test.
3. **Needs.** Define meaningful candidate-specific Deck Roles and relationships. Consider inputs, rewards, mana, access,
   interaction, protection, recovery and fallback routes where relevant. Set contextual coverage expectations and
   explain gaps. These prompts are not a mandatory role enum or a requirement for every generic support function.
4. **Options.** Retrieve missing functions and useful alternatives, then inspect relevant card facts. Explain retained
   options' contextual Card Contributions, including meaningful costs, conditions, timing, repeatability and limits.
   Reuse suitable returned facts. Combine seed/tag discovery with independent functional/Oracle searches; a tag match
   or popularity signal is neither admission evidence by itself nor a strategic strength score.
5. **Selection.** Choose a coherent configuration against the plan and practical Brief requirements. Distinguish active
   assessed alternatives from actual selected quantities. Pool presence does not authorize selection or establish
   Availability, affordability or selected coverage.
6. **Review.** Connect coverage, functional early actions, mana/curve, access, competing resources, dependencies,
   resilience and winning direction to the plan. Explain weak points, accepted compromises and uncertainty. A graph,
   histogram, role count or legality result alone cannot establish strategic adequacy, power or win rate.
7. **Retain.** Keep the affected current reasoning with its focused owner below, so later sessions can explain the
   configuration without reconstructing conversation history. Reconcile abandoned roles/options and relationships;
   keep desired unfilled functions as explained Role Coverage Gaps. Retain current strategy rather than an archive.

Provenance: this integrated sequence adopts the qualified activity → advantage → winning-direction framing and
whole-deck review from the [research synthesis](../../docs/research/2026-10-10-commander-deck-building-research.md),
including prior Commander Challenge notes. The [adopted methodology](https://github.com/OSmall/tomekin/issues/75#issuecomment-6234696206)
is the decision authority. Report-to-video mappings are qualified attribution, not exhaustive source certification.

## Count Qualified Contributions

Define what qualifies for each goal before counting it. Separate net card advantage from selection, temporary burst
mana from repeatable acceleration, and protection from removal. Selection or recursion can serve the plan without
being relabelled unconditional draw. Consider whether additional redundancy still improves the intended function.

Derive contributor quantities from actual selected membership in the goal's relevant sections, deduplicated per
Card Identity/role/section. Commander access is distinct from library copies. Sideboard membership and pool-only
alternatives supply zero active Mainboard coverage. Role totals can overlap and cannot be summed as deck size.

Review modal choices, shared costs and resource competition: a card assigned to two roles does not perform both
simultaneously. An expensive or conditional contribution is not equivalent to a cheap reliable one merely because its
label matches. Prefer contextual coverage assessments over fractional contribution weights, fixed strength scores or
a universal ratio. More role labels do not automatically make an option better.

Keep a desired unfilled role's gap explanation even when alternatives remain in the pool. Remove abandoned or unused
rotation roles from the current plan and reconcile affected options and relationships. Retained alternatives need a
genuinely justified contribution to a current meaningful role; resolve incompatible options deliberately rather than
inventing a role to keep them.

## Tune The Whole Contribution

Load the current rationale before restoring generic counts. Ordinary swaps preserve intended role density and
function: prefer like-for-like improvements where shape is healthy, then compare **every material lost and gained
contribution**, qualifications and affected relationships. A draw/sacrifice card replaced by draw-only support loses a
function even when both share the draw role.

Cross-role swaps can deliberately redistribute coverage. State the deficit/surplus or other reason, compensation and
trade-off; examine dependencies before cutting support for an attractive payoff. Coordinated swaps can preserve the
plan better than a forced single-card substitution. Review the actual aggregate result after partial acceptance.

Explain exact additions/cuts, quantities and sections, affected plan/Brief, benefit, coverage/dependencies, material
mana/curve and power/play-experience effects, and persistence target. Keep the explanation proportionate while retaining
complete underlying reasoning. Cutting selected copies retains a viable assessed alternative by default; deliberate
pool removal is a separate decision.

Resume current reasoning and useful facts. Material anchor, Brief, card-function, tag or methodology changes prompt
focused reassessment. Tag disappearance alone does not force a cut or erase reasoning. Record human observations with
their conditions and provenance; a small/selective sample does not prove execution or resilience. Static review and
Tomekin calculations are distinct from human gameplay, goldfishing and playtesting.

Provenance: whole-contribution tuning and contextual exceptions are adopted from the qualified report/correction,
not newly discovered omissions. Short plans, curve, enabler/payoff balance, non-stacking redundancy and anchor
compensation are review prompts. Universal step limits, compulsory low curves, drastic compensation ratios, fixed
five-to-seven-copy ceilings and mandatory goldfish sample sizes are outside the adopted method.

## Keep Reasoning With Its Owner

| Owner | Retained meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Brief | Intent, Format, constraints, power/play experience, preferences and Collection requirements. |
| Plan description | Current strategy, assumptions and unresolved strategic considerations. |
| Deck Role/goal/assessment | Function, relevant sections, qualifying criteria, contextual expectation, departure rationale and gaps. |
| Win Route/relationship | Intended outcome and priority rationale; meaningful enabling, enhancing, supporting or competing connection. |
| Pool option/contribution | Contextual rationale, qualifications, comparison and claim-local evidence. |
| Selected membership | Actual identities, sections and quantities; deck size and Portable Decklist authority. |

Use existing focused fields rather than duplicating a whole plan in every card note. Evidence identifies its source and
what it supports for that claim, retaining uncertainty and meaningful source differences. Card/reference facts remain
service-owned. Services enforce structure and explicitly requested deterministic checks; the agent owns contextual
adequacy, comparisons, explanation and confirmation. No executable effects model or strategic-validation service is
implied by this reasoning method.

## Use Bounded Statistics And Applicable Checks

For a supported calculation, identify the plan requirement, qualifying effects, population/sample/event and assumptions;
choose and explain a contextual reliability target, then interpret the result within those bounds. Distinguish exactly
from at-least events. There is no universal 70% threshold. Seeing a card does not establish mana, castability,
sequencing, opponent cooperation or winning. Exclude the report's inconsistent Sidisi percentages and turn guarantee
from operational evidence. Preserve the evaluator's factual land count and Mana Value summary alongside agent judgment.

This foundation supplies tool-independent reasoning. Use only tools actually exposed by the active adapter; it adds no
calculator API, candidate-edit API or automatic checks. The executable retrieval/edit/check/calculator examples and
four-workflow acceptance remain with [the integration request #64](https://github.com/OSmall/tomekin/issues/64).
Current lifecycle instructions govern available persistence; reasoning intended for focused records must not be
represented as already persisted when those operations are unavailable. In the adopted targeted-edit workflow,
authorized structural progress may save incomplete; checking is explicit and evidence reusable only while applicable.
Before claiming a completed deck result, perform applicable deterministic checks plus strategic, Brief and Collection
review. A strategic gap can be explained; applicable deterministic failure cannot be waived by prose.

Use authorization covering the actual result and target, explain material changes, and obtain agreement when it is
missing. A confirmed Brief alone does not authorize arbitrary swaps. Confirmation belongs to the lifecycle, without
per-node ceremonies or invented stored approval artifacts.
